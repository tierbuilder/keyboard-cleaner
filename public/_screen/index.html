    Lock your touchscreen and wipe it clean

    Screen Cleaner is a free tool that runs in your phone or tablet browser. It stops the screen reacting to taps and swipes and turns it black, so you can see every fingerprint, streak and speck of dust and wipe it away without opening apps, sending messages or changing settings by accident.

    1. Tap Start Cleaning Mode. The screen turns black and ignores touches.
    2. Find the smudges. Tilt the screen toward a window or lamp. On black, every mark stands out.
    3. Wipe. Use a soft, lint-free microfiber cloth, dry or slightly damp with water.
    4. Exit. Long-press the card on screen with one finger for 3 seconds, or wait for the timer.

    Why a black screen makes cleaning easier

    Fingerprints and oily streaks are almost invisible over a bright, busy screen. On a pure black screen with a light behind you, they show up clearly, so you know exactly where to wipe and when you're done. It also saves battery on OLED phones like recent iPhones and Samsung Galaxy models, where black pixels are switched off.

    How to clean an iPhone screen

    • Full black screen: iPhone browsers, including Safari and Chrome, only allow full screen for videos. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Screen Cleaner from your Home Screen for a screen with no address bar.
    • Lock the edges too: turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access), open Screen Cleaner, then triple-click the side button (or Home button). Triple-click again to end it.
    • Wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth, dry or slightly damp with water. Apple says a 70% isopropyl alcohol wipe is fine for the screen and back.
    • Avoid sprays, bleach, abrasive cleaners and paper towels. They wear down the fingerprint-resistant coating, and moisture can get into the speaker and charging port.

    How to clean an iPad screen

    • Open Screen Cleaner in Safari and tap Start Cleaning Mode. iPad goes full screen by itself.
    • If you use an Apple Pencil or keyboard case, take the iPad out first so you can reach the edges.
    • Wipe in straight lines from top to bottom, then turn the cloth to a dry side for a final pass to remove streaks.
    • Use Guided Access the same way as on iPhone if you want the edges and Home gesture locked too.

    How to clean an Android or Samsung Galaxy screen

    • Open Screen Cleaner in Chrome or Samsung Internet. It goes full screen and hides the address bar.
    • To stop swipes from the edges leaving the page, turn on app pinning (Settings → Security → More settings → App pinning) and pin your browser.
    • Wipe with a microfiber cloth. For a deeper clean, lightly dampen the cloth (never the phone) with water or a little 70% isopropyl alcohol.
    • On curved-edge screens, wipe along the curve rather than across it so the cloth doesn't catch.

    Cleaning a touchscreen laptop, Surface or Chromebook

    Screen Cleaner also works on touchscreen laptops. It blocks touches, the keyboard and the trackpad at the same time, so you can wipe the screen and the keys in one go. For tips on cleaning the keyboard itself, see Keyboard Cleaner.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I lock my phone screen to clean it?

    Open Screen Cleaner in your phone's browser and tap Start Cleaning Mode. The screen turns black and ignores taps and swipes until the timer ends or you long-press the card on screen for 3 seconds.

    Can I clean my phone screen without turning it off?

    Yes. Screen Cleaner keeps your phone on but stops it reacting to touches, and the black screen makes smudges easier to see than on a switched-off display.

    How do I exit Screen Cleaner?

    Long-press the card on screen with one finger for 3 seconds, or wait for the timer. Moving your finger, or touching in several places like a cloth does, cancels the long-press, so wiping can't exit by accident.

    Why can't it hide the address bar on iPhone?

    On iPhone, Safari, Chrome and every other browser only allow full screen for videos. Add Screen Cleaner to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there for a full black screen with no address bar. iPad and Android go full screen by themselves.

    Can I lock the whole iPhone screen, including the edges?

    Swipes from the screen edges and the side buttons are handled by iOS. Turn on Guided Access in Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the side or Home button while Screen Cleaner is open to lock every touch.

    Is it safe to use alcohol wipes on my phone screen?

    Apple says you can gently wipe iPhone and iPad screens with a 70 percent isopropyl alcohol wipe. Avoid bleach, abrasive cleaners, sprays and getting moisture into openings, since they can wear the fingerprint-resistant coating.

    Does it work on iPad, Android tablets and touchscreen laptops?

    Yes. It works on iPad, Android phones and tablets including Samsung Galaxy, and touchscreen laptops like Microsoft Surface and Chromebooks.

    Do I need to install an app?

    No. Screen Cleaner runs in your browser. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded. You can add it to your Home Screen if you want to open it like an app.
