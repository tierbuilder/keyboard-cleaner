Lock your keyboard and trackpad to clean them

Keyboard Cleaner is a free tool that runs in your browser. It locks your keyboard and trackpad so you can wipe away dust, crumbs and fingerprints without typing, clicking or swiping anything. There's nothing to install, no settings to change, and no need to turn off your laptop.

  1. Pick a timer. 30 seconds, 1, 2 or 5 minutes, or no limit.
  2. Click Start Cleaning Mode. The screen turns black, which also makes smudges on the display easy to spot.
  3. Wipe everything. Keys, trackpad and screen. Every key press, click and swipe is blocked.
  4. Unlock. Wait for the timer, or hold esc or press and hold the trackpad for 3 seconds.

How to clean a MacBook keyboard and trackpad

  • Start Keyboard Cleaner so nothing you touch triggers an action.
  • Use a soft, lint-free microfiber cloth, slightly damp with water. Never spray liquid directly onto the keyboard.
  • For germs, Apple says you can gently wipe the keyboard and trackpad with a 70% isopropyl alcohol wipe. Avoid bleach and getting moisture into openings.
  • Blow crumbs out from between keys with compressed air, holding the laptop at an angle.
  • Wipe the trackpad last, then the screen while it's still black so smudges stand out.

How to clean a Windows laptop keyboard

  • Turn on Fn Lock (often Fn + Esc) so the top row sends F-keys that Keyboard Cleaner can block.
  • Start Keyboard Cleaner in Chrome or Edge so the Windows key and Alt+Tab are blocked too.
  • Wipe the keys and touchpad with a slightly damp microfiber cloth, and use compressed air between keys.
  • Leave Ctrl+Alt+Del and the power button for last, since no app can block them.

Cleaning an external or mechanical keyboard

  • Keyboard Cleaner works with any keyboard plugged into or paired with your computer: Apple Magic Keyboard, Logitech, mechanical and gaming keyboards.
  • For a deep clean of a mechanical keyboard, unplug it and pull the keycaps. For a quick wipe, just lock it here and clean it where it sits.
  • Shake out crumbs upside down, then use compressed air at an angle between the switches.

Frequently asked questions

How do I lock my keyboard to clean it?

Open Keyboard Cleaner in your browser and click Start Cleaning Mode. Your keyboard and trackpad are locked until the timer ends, or until you hold esc or the trackpad for 3 seconds.

How do I temporarily disable my laptop keyboard to clean it?

You don't need to change any settings or install a driver. Keyboard Cleaner blocks every key press in your browser while you wipe, then gives the keyboard back when the timer ends.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Keyboard Cleaner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is installed, and nothing you type or click is uploaded.

Why do the volume and brightness keys still work?

macOS and Windows handle these keys before any browser sees them. On a Mac, turn on “Use F1, F2, etc. keys as standard function keys” in Keyboard settings. On Windows, turn on Fn Lock. Keyboard Cleaner then blocks them.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. It works on Mac and Windows in Chrome, Edge and Arc. Safari and Firefox work too, but can't block system shortcuts like ⌘Tab.

Can I clean my keyboard without turning off my computer?

Yes. Keyboard Cleaner locks the keyboard and trackpad while your computer stays on, so nothing happens when you wipe the keys.

How do I unlock it?

It unlocks on its own when the timer ends. To unlock early, hold esc or press and hold the trackpad for 3 seconds.

Can I use it to clean my phone or tablet screen?

Yes. For phones, iPads and touchscreens, use Screen Cleaner. It locks the touchscreen and turns it black so you can see and wipe away fingerprints.
